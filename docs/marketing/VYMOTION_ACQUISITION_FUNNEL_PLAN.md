## 1. Competitive read (Higgsfield & category)

### What Higgsfield does well

1. **Pricing page = paid ladder only**  
   Public pricing centers Starter / Plus / Ultra / Business. Free access exists as limited credits after signup, but it is **not** sold as a “Free plan card.” That keeps the page about money and value, not “$0 forever.”

2. **Outcome-led entry, not “Sign up”**  
   Product surfaces and affiliate content push **generate / create ads / Marketing Studio**, not account creation as the hero. Signup is the toll booth after intent.

3. **Job-to-be-done funnels**  
   Separate rails for UGC ads, product URL → ad, Soul face train, Cinema Studio. Each rail has its own promise and CTA. Visitors self-segment by *what they came to make*.

4. **Credit psychology**  
   Plans are framed as **credits → generations** (e.g. “~N videos”). Buyers understand burn rate before checkout.

5. **Creator-led distribution**  
   YouTube tutorials, affiliate deep links into Marketing Studio, “$100k ads with Higgsfield” content. Acquisition is product-demo content, not brand ads first.

### Where Vymotion can win (wedge vs Higgsfield)

| Higgsfield strength | Vymotion counter |
|---|---|
| Best-in-class raw gen tools | **End-to-end influencer business loop** (identity + studio + publish + earn) |
| Marketing Studio for DTC ads | **Persona consistency + monetization story** (brand deals, earnings) |
| High perceived spend / complex credit math | **Clear $5 Starter wedge** + simple credit strip |
| Tool for makers | **Studio for operators** who want an AI influencer that posts and earns |

**Positioning line (use in CTAs and ads):**  
> Not another generator — the studio to create an AI influencer that posts and earns.

## 2. Diagnosis of Vymotion today

| Issue | Why it hurts conversion |
|---|---|
| **Free plan on pricing grid** | Anchors buyers on $0; trained “browse only” as a plan; competes with Starter $5 |
| **Primary CTA = “Sign up”** | Account-first language; higher friction than benefit-first (“Start for free”) |
| **Funnel is flat** | Landing → signup → wall. Missing *aha* before paywall and job-based entry |
| **Pricing sells tiers, not jobs** | ICP (solo creator vs agency) should see different proof paths |
| **Free tier has 0 gen credits** | “Free” feels empty → bounce or never return |

### Funnel model we want (2026 PLG best practice)

Industry benchmarks (ChartMogul / SaaS conversion reports, 2026):

| Model | Visit → signup | Free → paid (typ.) | Fit for Vymotion |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Freemium (gated free forever) | High | 3–5% (good 8–12%) | Only if free has **real** gen taste |
| Free trial (no CC) | Medium | 4–6% good / 10–15% great | Strong if trial = full loop for N days/credits |
| Hybrid (soft free + paid ladder) | High | Best when “aha” then upgrade | **Recommended** |
| Reverse trial (paid features first → free) | Medium | Strong uplift | Phase 2 option |

**Recommendation:** Hybrid  
- **Marketing:** no Free *plan card* (paid ladder only — Higgsfield pattern).  
- **Product:** soft free explore + **signup bonus credits** or **timeboxed reverse-trial taste**.  
- **CTA language:** first visit **“Start for free”** (outcome), return **“Sign up” / “Log in”**.

### CTA language map

| Surface | First visit | Return / signed-out | Signed-in unpaid | Paying |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nav | **Start for free** | Sign up | Upgrade / Buy credits | Create |
| Pricing | Soft **Start for free** under cards | Same | Choose plan | Manage |
| Landing hero | **Start for free** | Same | Open studio | Dashboard |
| Empty gen state | Start for free → then paywall | — | Start Starter $5 | Top up |

Avoid “Sign up free” next to a Free plan — that double-free message devalues Starter.

## 5. Trending funnel plays to add (priority order)

### P0 — Product-led (ship next)

1. **Signup bonus credits (taste, not freemium forever)**  
   - e.g. 30–50 credits on first signup (enough for ~3–6 images).  
   - Cap: one per account; watermark optional on free taste.  
   - Goal: force aha in session 1 → paywall when dry.

2. **Job-based entry cards on landing**  
   - “Create an AI influencer”  
   - “Shoot photo content”  
   - “Make a short video”  
   - “Browse brand deals”  
   Each deep-links into create / studio / earnings with the same Start for free gate.

3. **Paywall UX, not dead ends**  
   - When credits = 0: modal with **Starter $5** primary + “See plans” secondary.  
   - Never only “contact us.”

### P1 — Conversion funnels (this month)

4. **Reverse trial (optional A/B)**  
   - Day 0–3: Creator-level models unlocked with small credit pool.  
   - Day 4: drop to explore-only or Starter prompt.  
   - Benchmark: reverse trials often lift paid conversion vs flat freemium.

5. **Template → clone funnel**  
   - “Clone Camila’s vibe in 2 minutes” / weekly featured templates.  
   - Higgsfield wins on empty canvas; we win on **guided persona**.

6. **Exit-intent / 2nd-session email**  
   - If signed up, 0 gens: email “Your first 3 shots — 1 click.”  
   - If generated, unpaid: “Keep this look — Starter $5.”

### P2 — Growth loops (months 2–3)

7. **UGC proof loop**  
   - Users export with subtle “made with Vymotion” only on free taste; paid = clean.  
   - Showcase gallery / Explore page (you already have mockups).

8. **Affiliate / creator program**  
   - Higgsfield grows via YouTubers. Offer 20–30% of first 3 months for creators who ship tutorials into `/create?ref=`.

9. **Agency path**  
   - Separate LP: multi-persona, commercial license, Pro/Studio.  
   - CTA: “Book a walkthrough” + self-serve Pro.

10. **Content SEO hubs**  
    - “AI influencer that makes money,” “consistent AI face,” “AI UGC without camera.”  
    - Avoid competitor model name SEO as primary brand story (GTM rule: finished product).

## 6. Messaging system (from a HF competitor PM)

### Do

- Lead with **outcome**: post-ready content, consistent identity, earn.  
- Price with **clarity**: $5 full loop; credits visible; failed gens free.  
- Social proof: real feed screenshots, time-to-first-post, earnings stories when real.  
- Annual toggle + “~2 months free” (already on pricing) — keep; it’s a standard SaaS conversion lever.

### Don’t

- Sell Free as a plan next to paid tiers.  
- Lead with model laundry lists (Soul, Seedance…) in cold ads.  
- “Sign up” as the only verb on first impression.  
- Promise unlimited free generation (margin death vs HF credit walls).

### Hero variants to test

| Variant | Line | Hypothesis |
|---|---|---|
| A | Start for free — create your first AI influencer | Volume of signups |
| B | From $5/mo — create, shoot, publish | Quality of paid intent |
| C | Your AI influencer. Photos, video, and posts — one studio | Clarity / bounce |
