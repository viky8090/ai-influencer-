# Part 1 — Strategy foundation

## 1.1 What Vymotion is (complete product)

**One-liner:**  
Create an AI influencer, generate scroll-stopping photos and video, and publish — all in Vymotion.

**Product story (use this everywhere):**  
Vymotion is a complete studio for AI influencers. You design a consistent persona, shoot photos and video in the Photo Studio and Video Studio, get AI help with captions and scripts, and publish on a paid plan. Plans use simple monthly credits. Failed generations are not charged.

**What the product does (user language only):**

| Step | Outcome |
|------|---------|
| Create | Consistent face, body, vibe, wardrobe |
| Generate | Photos + short video for feed/Reels/TikTok |
| Assist | Captions, backstory, scripts |
| Publish | Schedule/post (paid plans) |
| Scale | More influencers, more credits, commercial rights |

### Positioning rule (HARD)

Treat Vymotion as a **finished product**, not a wrapper, integrator, or “stack of tools.”

**Never say in marketing, ads, emails, captions, outreach, or support macros:**

- API keys, “bring your own account,” OAuth, middleware, wrapper
- Third-party model/vendor names or “powered by …”
- Technical plumbing of any kind

**Always say:** create · generate · publish · credits · plans · studio · influencer · identity consistency

## 1.4 ICP & anti-ICP

### Primary ICP — Maya (solo creator)
- Fashion, lifestyle, beauty, fitness, niche faceless pages
- Wants consistent posting without being on camera
- Price-sensitive; starts Free or Starter
- Discovers on TikTok / Instagram / YouTube Shorts

### Secondary ICP — Devon (agency) & Sam (marketer)
- Runs multiple personas or client content
- Cares about volume, commercial rights, reliability
- Discovers on LinkedIn / X / referrals / Product Hunt
- Converts more often to Creator / Pro

### Anti-ICP (do not target, do not optimize for)
- NSFW / deepfake harassment intent
- Pure freebie hunters who will never pay
- Enterprise RFPs (out of scope for this 90-day push)

## 1.5 Messaging pillars & hooks

### Brand pillars
1. **Consistent identity** — same face across thousands of posts  
2. **Speed** — idea → post-ready content in minutes  
3. **Complete studio** — photos + video + publish in one place  
4. **Honest pricing** — credits you can see; start at $5  
5. **Creator economics** — content is the product; brands pay for reach *(never guarantee income)*

### Scroll-stop hooks (short-form)
- “I built an influencer who posts while I sleep.”
- “No model. No camera. Still looks real.”
- “Full AI influencer studio from $5.”
- “How I make a week of content in one sitting.”
- “POV: your content calendar runs itself.”
- “She never cancels a shoot.”
- “Day 1 vs Day 30 of building an AI influencer.”
- “Stop renting freelancers for every UGC clip.”

### Soft CTA vs hard CTA
| Type | When | Copy pattern |
|------|------|----------------|
| Soft | Top-of-funnel / community | “Free account — create your influencer → vymotion.org” |
| Hard | Mid/bottom, retarget, email D7+ | “Unlock the full studio — Starter $5/mo” |
| Upsell | Active generators, brand deals | “Need commercial rights + more credits? Creator $29” |

### Forbidden claims
- Guaranteed followers, income, or brand deals  
- “Make $X/month with AI influencers” as a promise  
- Fake testimonials or invented metrics  
- Comparing competitors with false feature claims  

Use: *process, possibility, ranges, and disclaimers* (same spirit as the site FAQ).

## 1.6 Conversion system (high conversion)

```
Attention (content / social / launch)
    → Landing or in-bio link (UTM)
    → Free signup
    → Activation: create first influencer + first generation
    → Value moment (save / export / schedule intent)
    → Paywall or credit need
    → Starter $5
    → Habit (weekly gens)
    → Creator $29 (commercial / volume)
```

### Activation definition (must hit before pushing hard pay)
**Activated** = created ≥1 influencer **and** completed ≥1 successful generation.

### Free → paid conversion design
| Lever | Action |
|-------|--------|
| Price | $5 Starter removes “is this worth it?” friction |
| Clarity | Every generate action shows credit cost |
| Proof | Demo feed of a real Vymotion persona |
| Speed | 5-minute “first post” checklist in product/email |
| Scarcity (ethical) | Founding-member bonus credits, not fake countdown timers |
| Upsell timing | After multi-influencer or brand-deal intent |

### Email / lifecycle skeleton (D0–D21)

| Day | Goal | Message focus |
|-----|------|----------------|
| D0 | Welcome | What Vymotion is + 3-step checklist (create → generate → save) |

# Part 5 — Content engine

## 5.1 Dogfood the product
All demo influencers, photos, and videos should be **made in Vymotion**. The product is the ad. Show UI briefly (honest, not fake polish).

## 5.2 Cadence (minimum)
- **Daily:** 1–2 short videos → TikTok + IG + YT Shorts  
- **Weekly:** 1 long YouTube or carousel deep-dive; 2 threads; 3 LinkedIn  
- **Batch:** 1 production day → 7–14 days of assets  

## 5.3 Content pillars (rotate)
| # | Pillar | Example |
|---|--------|---------|
| 1 | Process / transformation | Create influencer → first shoot in one video |
| 2 | Persona day-in-life | “Monday with [AI persona]” |

```
1 long recording
```

# Part 6 — KPIs, reporting, behind-pace

## 6.1 Weekly dashboard (every Monday)

| Metric | M1 target | M2 target | M3 target |
|--------|----------:|----------:|----------:|
| Free signups / week | 150 | 350 | 500 |
| Activation rate | ≥40% | ≥45% | ≥50% |
| Free → paid (30d cohort) | ≥6% | ≥8% | ≥10% |
| New paid / week | 20 | 40–45 | 60+ |
| Content posts / week | 10+ | 14+ | 14–21 |
| Blended CAC | &lt;$12 | &lt;$10 | &lt;$10 |
| Paid churn (monthly) | track | &lt;8% | &lt;8% |
| Top hook (views → signup) | log | log | log |

## 6.2 Experiment log
For every test: **hypothesis · channel · metric · sample size · kill/keep rule · result**.

Example kill rule: “If Reddit posts get &lt;2 signups per 5 posts, pause Reddit creation; keep comments only.”

## 6.3 Behind-pace protocol
Trigger: week’s new paid **&lt; 70%** of target **or** cumulative trajectory misses month goal.

Do in order for 7 days:
1. **Activation only** — email/push all free users who never generated.  
2. **Starter flash** — clear $5 offer + bonus credits (real, time-boxed once).  
3. **Affiliate + micro-creator sprint** — 20 new outreach/day.  
4. **Boost top 3 organic creatives** only (small budget).  
5. **Pause** low-ROI channels to free time.  
6. **Agency DM sprint** for Creator/Pro (higher ARPU closes gap faster).

## 6.4 Risks & mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Abuse / credit cost overrun | Plan gates, rate limits, monitor free→gen abuse |
| Platform spam bans | Value-first, native posting, no cross-post spam floods |
| Income-claim backlash | Disclaimers; process content; no guarantees |
| Likeness / ethics | Original personas only; policy-aligned content |
| Weak demos | Only ship content from real product generations |
| Burnout | Batch content; reuse system; agent-assisted drafts |
