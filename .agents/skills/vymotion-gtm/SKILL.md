# Vymotion GTM Operator

## Overview

Execute Vymotion marketing as a finished product: AI influencer studio at `https://vymotion.org`. Goal: 500 paid members in 90 days. Primary wedge: Starter `$5/mo`; upsell: Creator `$29/mo`.

## Hard Positioning Rules

Always say: create, generate, publish, credits, plans, studio, influencer, identity consistency.

Never say in marketing copy: API keys, bring your own account, OAuth, middleware, wrapper, model/vendor names, powered by, plumbing.

Never promise guaranteed followers, income, brand deals, or fake testimonials.

## Funnel

Attention → landing page with UTM → free signup → activation → value moment → Starter `$5` → usage habit → Creator `$29`.

Activated user = created at least 1 influencer and completed at least 1 successful generation.

## Weekly Operating Loop

1. Monday: update KPI dashboard.
2. Pick top 3 hooks by views → clicks → signups.
3. Produce 7–14 days of short-form assets.
4. Post daily on TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts.
5. Reuse winners for X, LinkedIn, Pinterest, Reddit value answers.
6. DM creators/agencies from current sprint target.
7. Friday: kill weak channels, double winning hooks.

## Content Formula

Short video:
1. First 1.5s: visual result + hook text.
2. Show product process briefly.
3. Show output.
4. CTA: free signup or Starter `$5`.

Caption:
1. Hook line.
2. Concrete outcome.
3. CTA.
4. 3–8 relevant hashtags.

## CTA Rules

Soft CTA: `Free account — create your influencer → vymotion.org`

Hard CTA: `Unlock the full studio — Starter $5/mo`

Upsell CTA: `Need commercial rights + more credits? Creator $29`

## KPI Targets

Month 1 weekly: 150 free signups, 40% activation, 20 paid, 10+ posts, CAC `< $12`.

Month 2 weekly: 350 free signups, 45% activation, 40–45 paid, 14+ posts, CAC `< $10`.

Month 3 weekly: 500 free signups, 50% activation, 60+ paid, 14–21 posts, CAC `< $10`.

## Behind-Pace Protocol

Trigger: new paid < 70% of weekly target or monthly trajectory missed.

Run for 7 days:
1. Email/push free users who never generated.
2. Starter `$5` flash with real bonus credits.
3. 20 affiliate/micro-creator outreaches per day.
4. Boost top 3 organic creatives only.
5. Pause low-ROI channels.
6. Run agency DM sprint for Creator/Pro.
